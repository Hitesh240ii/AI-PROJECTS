ACME CORP - EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY (SAMPLE, FICTIONAL)

1. Annual leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year; any additional unused days expire on 31 March.

2. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

3. How to request leave
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Requests are approved by the employee's line manager. Any request longer than 10 consecutive working days must also be approved by the Department Head.

4. Parental leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks. Leave may be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
